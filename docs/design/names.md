# Naming, round 2: replacement for "SignalFeed"

> Round 1 (Offing, Kenning, Trigpoint and others) was rejected. None of those names had a free exact-match .com, and none stood out.
> Round 2 rule: the exact name + ".com" must be free at standard price. No prefixes, suffixes, .co or .io.
> Checked 17 November 2026.

## Method

I brainstormed about 500 candidates in three styles: (a) two-word compounds, (b) short invented or blended words, and (c) real words or idioms with a small twist. I bulk-checked the .com for all of them with Vercel's registry lookup.

- Real words and short invented words (5 to 6 letters) were almost all taken: Fundra, Kapita, Raiso, Tidings, Mintage, Upraise and many more.
- About 75 names came back available. Nearly all are compounds built on raise, round or seed.
- I ranked those 75 for quality, ran web searches on the top 22, and checked the price of the 12 finalists.

**Every finalist's .com costs $12.03 a year to register and $12.03 to renew. None is premium-priced.** This is not a legal clearance. Search UKIPO classes 35, 36 and 42 before committing.

**Dropped after vetting:**
- **Seedford:** [SeedFord Partners](https://venturecapitalarchive.com/venture-funds/seedford-partners-seedford-partners-com) is a VC firm.
- **Raiseline:** taken by [Raiseline Ventures](https://crunchbase.com/organization/raiseline-ventures).
- **Seedtable and Seedscope:** existing startup-data brands.

**Shared risk for "Raise-" names:** the prefix is crowded in fundraising tools. Examples are [Raise AI](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/raise-ai-ltd), [RaiseNow](https://craft.co/raisenow) and [Raisewell Ventures](https://crunchbase.com/organization/raisewell-ventures). There is also raise(fn), a Show HN project that tracks funding rounds. A raise- compound is distinct as a whole word, but it is weaker to trademark than a coined word.

## (a) Compounds

### Roundvane
- **.com:** roundvane.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** A weathervane for funding rounds: it shows which way the money is blowing.
- **Both audiences:** Market readers see which sectors the wind favours. Sellers follow the vane to where money has just landed.
- **Tagline:** *See which way the money blows.*
- **Conflicts:** No exact match found. The nearest results were unrelated hardware ([Hull Vane](https://village.ai/company/hull-vane-b-v-)). It avoids the crowded "raise" prefix.
- **Risk:** Low

### Raisechart
- **.com:** raisechart.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** A chart of every raise, in both senses: a graph and a navigator's chart.
- **Both audiences:** Market readers get the trend charts. Sellers get a map to plot a course to companies.
- **Tagline:** *Every raise, charted.*
- **Conflicts:** No exact match found. Near names: [RaiseNow](https://craft.co/raisenow), a donations platform, plus the shared raise- note.
- **Risk:** Low to medium

### Raisetrove
- **.com:** raisetrove.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** A trove is a found hoard of treasure. Here, a store of newly funded companies.
- **Both audiences:** It leans towards finding companies (sellers). For market readers, the trove is the dataset.
- **Tagline:** *Find the newly funded.*
- **Conflicts:** No exact match found. Several unrelated "Trove" startups exist ([Trove resale](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/trove-2), [Trove Finance](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/trove-finance)).
- **Risk:** Low to medium

### Raisecurrent
- **.com:** raisecurrent.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** The current of capital, and the current (latest) raises.
- **Both audiences:** "Current" covers both the flow of money (trends) and what is happening now (fresh leads).
- **Tagline:** *Where capital is flowing now.*
- **Conflicts:** No exact match found. It is long (four syllables), which weakens it as a wordmark.
- **Risk:** Low

## (b) Invented or blended

### Novround
- **.com:** novround.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** From the Latin *novus*, new, plus "round": new rounds.
- **Both audiences:** Market readers see new rounds as they land. Sellers find newly funded teams.
- **Tagline:** *New rounds, first.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. The search returned only November tournament rounds. Possible issues: "Nov" may read as November, and it needs one hearing to spell.
- **Risk:** Low

### Kenround
- **.com:** kenround.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** "Ken" (to know, a Scots and English word) plus "round". It keeps the best of round 1's Kenning.
- **Both audiences:** Know the rounds: market knowledge, and knowing who just raised.
- **Tagline:** *Know every round.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. The nearest result was [Kenya Seed Company](https://village.ai/company/kenya-seed-company), in another sector.
- **Risk:** Low

### Raisewick
- **.com:** raisewick.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** "-wick" is the Old English suffix for a settlement or trading place, as in Warwick and Keswick. So: the place where raises gather. It sounds like a real British place.
- **Both audiences:** It is a hub where market readers browse and sellers come to trade.
- **Tagline:** *Where the UK's raises gather.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. The nearest was [Raiseworks](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/raiseworks), a US lending fintech, now acquired. The raise- note applies.
- **Risk:** Low to medium

### Raisory
- **.com:** raisory.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** "Raise" plus "-ory", as in advisory or directory: a directory of raises.
- **Both audiences:** It suggests both a reference (market) and a directory (finding companies).
- **Tagline:** *The directory of who raised.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. Near names are [Raisy UK Ltd](https://raisy-uk-ltd.ltds.uk/) and [Raisio](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/raisio/), a Finnish food company. Its pronunciation ("RAY-zuh-ree") is less obvious than the others.
- **Risk:** Medium

## (c) Twists on real words and idioms

### Raisewind
- **.com:** raisewind.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** "To raise the wind" is an old British idiom for getting hold of money you need. It is nautical in tone and literally about raising funds.
- **Both audiences:** Market readers see who raised the wind and where. Sellers catch the tailwind behind newly funded companies.
- **Tagline:** *Who's raised the wind.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. The nearest was [Wind.app](https://startupintros.com/news/2023-11-27-wind-app-pre-seed). A small risk: someone may joke about "wind".
- **Risk:** Low

### Raisefall
- **.com:** raisefall.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** A twist on "windfall": money that has just landed.
- **Both audiences:** For sellers, a raise is a client's windfall. Market readers see where money falls.
- **Tagline:** *Track every windfall.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. The nearest are [Rise & Fall](https://startup-seeker.com/company/riseandfall~co), a homeware brand, and [Raise Financial](https://www.cbinsights.com/investor/raise-financial). "Fall" could read as falling. That is a mild negative in a markets product.
- **Risk:** Low to medium

### Raisevine
- **.com:** raisevine.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** A twist on "heard it on the grapevine": the news of who raised, but verified.
- **Both audiences:** Market readers get the news. Sellers hear about raises before rivals do.
- **Tagline:** *Heard it on the raisevine. Then confirmed it.*
- **Conflicts:** No exact match. A wine-trade platform called RAISE (raise.wine) is in a different sector.
- **Risk:** Low

### Foreround
- **.com:** foreround.com, available, $12.03 a year
- **Story:** A twist on "foreground": the rounds that matter, brought forward.
- **Both audiences:** It puts the important raises up front, which is the brief's own goal (problem 3: rows don't show what matters).
- **Tagline:** *The rounds that matter, up front.*
- **Conflicts:** No match. The nearest was [Front Runner App](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/front-runner-app). It may be misheard as "foreground", so it would need the logo to carry it.
- **Risk:** Low

---

## Top 4 across styles

1. **Roundvane (a).** It is the most distinctive name on the list and avoids the crowded "raise" prefix. The weathervane image suits market readers and gives an easy logo mark (an arrow, a vane). Its search was clean.
2. **Kenround (b).** It is short and British, with a clear meaning ("know the rounds"). It keeps what was liked in Kenning, and its search was clean.
3. **Raisewind (c).** It is a real British money idiom that most people won't know, so it rewards the story on the About page. It sounds nautical and premium.
4. **Novround (b).** It is the most literal of the four ("new rounds"). It is short and easy to spell once heard, and good if clarity matters more than character.
