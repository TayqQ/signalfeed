Construction

  • Ring: radius 13 and stroke 5.5 in a 48 grid, with round ends. The gap is 64°, centred on the top right (45°).
  • Dot: radius 4.75, on the 45° line, 20 from the ring's centre. Always orange.
  • Optical centre: the ring and dot together sit centred in the box (grey dashed line), not the ring alone.
  • Clear space: at least one dot diameter on every side (orange dashed line).
  • Lockup: the mark is 1.18 times the cap height, centred on the capitals, with a gap of 0.38 times the cap height.
  • Don't: move the dot or the gap, recolour the dot, box the mark, or add a shadow, gradient or stretch.
