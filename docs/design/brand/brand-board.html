Raisory: brand directions

Three logo directions on one shared palette (deep green, warm off-white and a brass accent) and one type system. Use the toggle to see dark mode. These are working drafts: the chosen mark gets refined and drawn as final SVG paths.

1 · Institution

Serif wordmark, step mark

Three rising bars stand for the funding rounds stepping up, and the newest round is picked out in brass. The serif wordmark reads like a fund house or the FT: established and trustworthy. The mark doubles as a small chart.

Feel: premium, institutional
